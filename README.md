# ZeroBloatTools — Free Client-Side Utilities

> **Live Website:** [https://zerobloattools.com](https://zerobloattools.com)

A collection of 12 specialized browser-based tools, calculators, and interactive visualizers. Everything runs 100% client-side in the browser with zero server uploads, no logins, and zero paywalls.

---

### ⚡ Home Trades & Electrical
* **[3-Way & 4-Way Switch Wiring Simulator](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/trades/3-way-switch-simulator)** — Interactive AC circuit visualizer demonstrating hot legs, travelers, and common terminal states in real time.
* **[Stair Stringer Calculator & 2D Visualizer](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/trades/stair-stringer-calculator)** — Calculates stair risers, tread runs, and 2x12 cut angles with IRC/IBC code validation and fraction tape conversion.
* **[Home EV Charger & Panel Load Calculator](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/trades/ev-panel-load-calculator)** — Residential electrical panel capacity solver (100A/150A/200A) under NEC Article 220.82 with Smart Splitter bypass rules.

### 💻 Developer & DevOps
* **[cURL to Playwright & k6 Script Converter](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/developer/curl-to-playwright)** — Converts raw cURL commands directly into clean Playwright API tests and Grafana k6 load scripts with automated client-side secret masking.

### 📦 Shipping & E-Commerce
* **[4×6 Thermal Label Auto-Cropper & PDF Rotator](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/shipping/thermal-label-cropper)** — Crops full-sheet 8.5×11 PDF shipping labels to 4×6 direct thermal format locally in browser memory for Rollo, Munbyn, and Zebra printers.

### 🚗 Automotive & RV
* **[CAT Scale Trailer Tongue Weight Calculator](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/automotive/tongue-weight-calculator)** — 2-pass and 3-pass CAT scale ticket solver with trailer sway risk gauge and WDH restoral checks.
* **[Camper Van & Marine Solar Electrical Architect](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/automotive/van-solar-calculator)** — Sizes solar panels, LiFePO4 battery banks, MPPT controllers, and inverters with AWG wire gauge drop calculations.

### 💰 Real Estate & Finance
* **[BRRRR Strategy Investment Deal Analyzer](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/finance/brrrr-deal-analyzer)** — Models rehab, holding costs, refinance equity pull, and DSCR qualification with 1-click printable bank sheets.

### 🧬 Health & Science
* **[Peptide Reconstitution & Syringe Dosage Visualizer](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/health/peptide-calculator)** — Calculates microgram/milligram peptide reconstitution with an interactive animated SVG syringe plunger (U-100, U-50, U-30).

### ⚖️ Legal & Compliance
* **[California Final Paycheck Waiting Time Penalty Calculator](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/legal/ca-final-paycheck-penalty)** — Computes statutory penalties under California Labor Code § 203 with an automated formal demand letter generator.

### 🎵 Audio & Acoustics
* **[Audio Delay, Reverb & LFO Tempo Studio](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/audio/delay-bpm-calculator)** — Exact milliseconds, LFO frequencies (Hz), and DAW sample offsets across sample rates with live tap-tempo auditioning.

### 🖼️ Optics & Photography
* **[Picture Frame Mat Board Studio](https://zerobloattools.com/tools/photography/frame-mat-calculator)** — Calculates mat borders, window openings, and bottom-weighted optical center offsets with bevel cutter guides.

---

### Architecture & Privacy
* **100% Client-Side:** All image processing, calculations, and conversions happen locally on your device.
* **Zero Tracking:** No analytics cookies, no user tracking, no signups required.
* **Fast Edge Delivery:** Served globally via Cloudflare Pages static edge network.
